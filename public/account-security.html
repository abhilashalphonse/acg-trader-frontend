Account access and identity

Know where you are signing in.

ACG Trader is a trading and simulated-market platform. ACG Trader's Firebase Hosting address is acg-trader.web.app. The platform is developed by ACG-MANAGEMENT, UNIPESSOAL LDA, Portugal.

Existing ACG Funded trading accounts

The ACG Trader login accepts a trading account ID and the trading-account password issued for that account by ACG Funded. Check your own account with ACG Funded before entering trading credentials. This is not a Google, email-provider, banking or payment-service login form.

Connect Prop and other providers

The Connect Prop form also requests a provider or organization identifier. Enter a trading-account password only if it was issued by your authorized trading provider for use with ACG Trader. Do not enter unrelated account passwords.

Independent personal account sign-in

Where personal registration is enabled, ACG Trader uses Supabase authentication for its own email-based accounts. Selecting “Continue with Google” redirects to the Google sign-in provider; the ACG Trader form does not ask for your Google password. Personal demo accounts are simulated and do not represent cash deposits or funded brokerage balances.

Protect your account

  • Check the browser address bar before entering any credentials.
  • Never share one-time codes, recovery links, banking passwords, wallet seed phrases or Google account passwords with a trading-account login form.
  • If you encounter a browser security warning, do not bypass it. Contact the organization that issued your trading credentials through its independently verified website.

ACG Trader does not request bank passwords or wallet recovery phrases to access a trading workspace. If a page asks for these, stop and verify its origin.
