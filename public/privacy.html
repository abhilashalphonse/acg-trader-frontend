Privacy at ACG Trader.

ACG Trader is trading technology developed by ACG-MANAGEMENT, UNIPESSOAL LDA, Portugal. This notice describes the categories of information handled by ACG Trader’s platform. Account providers may have their own privacy terms for the accounts they issue.

Information used by the service

Depending on your account type, the platform handles trading-account identifiers and credentials submitted for authentication, account and session identifiers, personal-account email addresses when registration is enabled, and simulated order, position and workspace activity. Technical information such as connection details and error or security logs may also be processed when operating the service.

Why information is used

These details support authentication, authorized account access, account and session security, simulated trading workflows, service diagnostics, and prevention of unauthorized activity. ACG Trader login does not ask for banking passwords, Google account passwords, or wallet recovery phrases.

Infrastructure and sign-in providers

ACG Trader uses Railway for its web application and trading backend and Supabase for supported personal-account authentication. Accounts issued by ACG Funded or another authorized trading organization may also involve that account issuer’s systems and terms.

Storage and access

Trading-session refresh credentials are maintained through protected backend cookies. Personal-account provider tokens used for browser authentication are held in browser memory during the session. Additional account, trading and diagnostic records may be retained by the relevant systems; retention periods and applicable legal bases depend on the information and account relationship.

Privacy questions and requests

For an account issued through ACG Funded, use its published support channel identified on our Contact page. For accounts issued by another organization, contact that issuer regarding its account records. Requests about platform-operated information should identify the account type and concern without including passwords or security codes.

Important: This platform-specific information does not replace the separate privacy notices and contractual obligations of account issuers, where applicable. The operator must verify formal data-controller contact information, retention schedules and rights procedures before treating this text as a complete legal compliance assessment.
