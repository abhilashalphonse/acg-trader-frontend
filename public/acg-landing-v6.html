ACG Trader FAQs

What is ACG Trader?

ACG Trader is a chart-first trading technology platform for market analysis, risk-based order planning and simulated account execution. It is developed by ACG-MANAGEMENT, UNIPESSOAL LDA, Portugal.

Is ACG Trader a broker?

No. ACG Trader provides trading technology and simulated trading environments. It is not a broker or investment adviser, does not hold client trading deposits, and does not currently offer live brokerage execution.

How do I access my trading account?

For an ACG Funded account, use the trading-account ID and password issued for that account. If another integrated organization issued your account, use its authorized provider identifier and trading credentials. Never enter unrelated email, Google or banking passwords. Learn about account access.

Can I trade with real money on ACG Trader?

The public ACG Trader platform currently offers simulated trading through authorized accounts, not live brokerage trading. Simulated balances, positions and results do not represent actual deposits or assets. Read the service terms.

Which markets are available?

Supported simulated environments may include forex, selected commodities and cryptocurrencies. The instruments you can view or trade depend on your account permissions, data coverage and your provider. Website videos and market movements are illustrative, not a live list of available markets.

Can I connect an AI assistant to ACG Trader?

The AI-assistant connection shown on this website is an illustrative concept, not a promise that third-party integrations are available for every account. AI outputs can be inaccurate, and users must review and authorize their own trading decisions.

Where can I get help with my account?

Contact the organization that issued your trading account. For ACG Funded-issued accounts, use its verified support channel. For another provider, contact that organization's official support team. Never send passwords or recovery codes. See account-support information.

Terms & risk notice. ACG Trader provides market-analysis technology and simulated trading, not brokerage services or investment advice. Simulated balances, fills and results are not real funds or guaranteed outcomes. Market data, AI outputs and website illustrations may be delayed, inaccurate or illustrative. Trading leveraged instruments in real markets can involve substantial financial losses. Available services and account rules depend on your provider.
