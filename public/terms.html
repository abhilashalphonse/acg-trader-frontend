Using ACG Trader.

ACG Trader is a trading technology platform developed by ACG-MANAGEMENT, UNIPESSOAL LDA, Portugal. These terms describe the intended use of its public interface and authorized trading workspaces. Where access is issued by a trading organization, its additional account terms may also apply.

Authorized account access

Use only your own credentials or credentials legitimately issued to you by an authorized account provider. An ACG Funded trading-account login and a separate personal-account login are different sign-in processes. Do not submit unrelated Google, email, bank or wallet credentials to a trading-account login form.

Simulated trading and market information

ACG Trader includes simulated trading environments. Simulated balances, fills, positions and results are not live brokerage assets or guarantees of actual market execution. Website interface illustrations contain sample prices and values and must not be relied upon as live market data.

Information, not investment advice

Platform tools, charts and educational descriptions are informational and technological services. They do not constitute individualized investment advice or a guarantee of gains. Trading leveraged instruments can involve substantial financial risk when conducted in real markets.

Service availability and product roadmap

Available instruments and features depend on account permissions, service status and market-data rights. Futures, Options, ACG Trader Cloud and White Label are described as planned or in development unless explicitly made available under a separate written arrangement.

Account security and appropriate use

Keep login details confidential, follow your account issuer’s rules and do not attempt to access another user’s account or bypass platform protections. If a browser displays a security warning, do not bypass it.

Questions and provider-specific terms

See Contact for verified support information for ACG Funded-issued accounts. If your trading account was issued by another organization, review its own terms and support arrangements.
