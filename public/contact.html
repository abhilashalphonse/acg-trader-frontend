Support and enquiries

Get the right account support.

ACG Trader is developed by ACG-MANAGEMENT, UNIPESSOAL LDA, Portugal. For account support, start with the organization that issued your trading credentials.

ACG Funded-issued trading accounts

ACG Funded lists support for ACG Trader platform access and trading-account questions. Use its published support email: support@acgfunded.com.

That email is the ACG Funded support channel for connected accounts; it should not be confused with an independent legal-contact address for ACG Trader’s Portuguese operator. You can also verify the channel on ACG Funded’s official website.

Accounts issued by another organization

If your account was issued by a different prop firm or trading organization, contact its official support team for credential issuance, account access and entitlement questions. Verify its domain independently before sharing account identifiers.

What to include

Provide a general description of the issue and, where relevant, your account or challenge identifier. Never email passwords, recovery codes, bearer tokens, wallet seed phrases or private keys.

General platform and privacy enquiries

Questions about ACG Trader’s operator, product availability or privacy should be directed through a verified contact channel supplied by your service or account agreement. A standalone ACG Trader corporate contact address has not yet been published on this page.
